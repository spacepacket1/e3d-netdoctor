e3d-netdoctor Point it at your network. Get told why it's slow.

Why this exists

Every network engineer and Wireshark user has had this conversation: “Is it my wifi, or is it Comcast again?” That question is answerable from a single packet capture — if you know to group retransmissions and RTT outliers by destination and by local device, and weigh provider diversity instead of raw destination count. Most people don't do that math by hand. netdoctor does, every time, and shows its work.

VerdictMeans
Likely Upstream/ISPSignal spread across many unrelated destinations and providers — not one device
Likely LocalSignal concentrated on one local device (MAC) across many destinations — bad wifi, flaky NIC, bad cabling
Likely Destination/Path-SpecificSignal confined to one or two destinations while everything else is clean
